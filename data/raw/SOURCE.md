# ACSDT1Y2025.C16001-Chicago.csv

- **Table:** C16001, Language Spoken at Home for the Population 5 Years and Over
- **Dataset:** U.S. Census Bureau, 2025 American Community Survey 1-Year Estimates
- **Geography:** Chicago city, Illinois
- **Downloaded:** 2026-10-31 (via data.census.gov)
- **Used in:** `lib/citywide.ts`

Values are survey estimates with margins of error, not exact counts. The margin of
error on the non-English total (about ±23,625) is derived from the published margins
for "Total" and "Speak only English": sqrt(6,201² + 22,797²).

# CMAP-Community-Data-Snapshots-2026.csv

- **Table:** C16001 (language spoken at home, population 5 years and over), 2020–2024 American Community Survey 5-year estimates, as published by CMAP
- **Publisher:** Chicago Metropolitan Agency for Planning (CMAP), Community Data Snapshots (CCA 2026), one row per community area (77)
- **Downloaded:** 2026-11-02
- **Used in:** `lib/area-data.ts` (generated by `scripts/build-area-data.py`)
- **Field dictionary:** `CMAP-CDS-Field-Descriptions-Crosswalk.csv`

Columns used: `GEOID` (community area number), `POP_OV5`, `ENGLISH`, `NOT_ENGLISH`, `LING_ISO`
(English less than "very well"), and the language groups `SPANISH`, `SLAVIC`, `CHINESE`, `TAGALOG`,
`ARABIC`, `KOREAN`, `OTHER_ASIAN`, `OTHER_EURO`, `OTHER_UNSPEC`.

Notes:
- CMAP assigned census tracts to community areas, so some counts are fractional; percentages are computed from them and rounded to 0.1.
- The file has no margins of error. CMAP notes that 5-year ACS data is sample-based, so margins of error can be large for low-population areas.
- Checks run: language groups sum to `NOT_ENGLISH` in all 77 areas; `ENGLISH + NOT_ENGLISH = POP_OV5`; area names match the site's list; the 77-area total is about 908,000 non-English speakers (35.4%), versus about 913,000 in the 2025 1-year citywide file.
